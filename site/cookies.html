Cookies

This website sets no cookies and does no tracking.

On this website

None. Not one, first-party or otherwise, and nothing is kept in your browser's storage either. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to. The pages are static files with a stylesheet, two fonts, a few images and a scripted demo, all served from this domain, plus the Lightmorphic apps menu in the corner, which is a script and some icons from apps.lightmorphic.com. That is ours, it sets no cookies and it stores nothing.

There is no analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no embedded video and no social widget. Nothing on these pages reports back to us or to anyone else.

In the Fetch Terminal app

The app is a desktop program, not a website, so cookies do not apply to it. What it does keep on your own disk is listed on the privacy page.
